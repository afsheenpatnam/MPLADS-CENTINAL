ANAMOLIES:

⏱️ Time Anomaly Detection
Detects unusual project delays by comparing actual progress against planned milestones, identifying projects at risk of exceeding scheduled completion timelines.
💰 Cost Anomaly Detection
Analyzes project expenditure patterns to identify abnormal spending, budget overruns, inflated invoices, and potential fund misuse.
🏗️ Work Anomaly Detection
Verifies whether reported work progress matches on-ground execution using GPS logs, image evidence, and activity updates to detect fake or incomplete work.
FRAUD:
📋 Sanction Report Verification
Validates whether project activities, expenditures, and progress align with the approved sanction report, ensuring compliance with sanctioned objectives, budget allocations, and timelines.


DETECT INEFFICIENCES:

🏗️ Plan vs Construction Inefficiency
Identifies deviations between the approved project plan and the actual construction executed on-site, ensuring that all sanctioned specifications and deliverables are properly implemented.
🔍 Quality Check
Evaluates the quality of construction materials and workmanship to ensure compliance with government standards, safety regulations, and project requirements.
📦 Quantity Check
Verifies whether the reported quantity of materials and completed work matches the actual on-site usage and construction progress.

FRAUD:

📋 Sanction Report Verification
Validates whether project activities, expenditures, and progress align with the approved sanction report, ensuring compliance with sanctioned objectives, budget allocations, and timelines.
📑 Condition Compliance Monitoring
Continuously checks whether contractors and project stakeholders adhere to all government regulations, approval conditions, quality standards, and contractual obligations throughout project execution.
💰 Expenditure Anomaly
A significant difference has been detected between the project expenditure and the actual work completed, indicating potential financial inefficiencies or irregularities.
📍 Scheduled Site Attendance Monitoring
The system requires contractors to report at the project site on pre-assigned dates and times defined by the administration. Attendance is verified using GPS-based check-ins and geofencing mechanisms.
